About RESQAI

RESQAI — “AI That Saves Lives When Every Second Counts” — is an AI-powered disaster response and emergency coordination project concept. The planned platform brings together SOS alerts, GPS-based location sharing, disaster warnings, safe-route guidance, nearby hospitals and shelters, and communication between people and response teams.

Important: This is a student project demo. SOS does not alert rescue teams, and no live disaster feed, hospital verification, or backend dispatch system is connected. In a real emergency in India, call 112 directly and follow official local instructions.
RESQAI • Student project prototype • CSE (AI & ML), VBIT
Built to demonstrate an emergency coordination interface; not a substitute for official emergency services.
